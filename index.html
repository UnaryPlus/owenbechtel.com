Welcome to my website. I am a first-year PhD student studying mathematics at the University of Massachusetts Amherst. I have bachelor's degrees in pure mathematics and computer science, with a minor in linguistics. I created this website when I was in high school, and many of the things on here date from that time.

Some of my online accounts are called "UnaryPlus", which refers to the positive sign in expressions like "+1". One of my hobbies is constructing fictional languages; I am currently working on a language called Kalvaszti, which I hope to finish within the next few months. I also plan on getting back into writing music soon, which is something I have not done for a few years.

I have experience in many programming languages, including Python, Javascript, Typescript, C, C++, and Java. My favorite programming language is Haskell, and I have several packages published on Hackage, the central Haskell package archive.
